Uganda (UG)

Quick facts

Section titled “Quick facts”
FieldValue
Drive sideLeft-hand
Language(s)English
RegulatorMinistry of Works and Transport
StandardBritish-derived (Traffic and Road Safety Act)

The @road-signs/ug package contains Uganda road signs (British-derived (Traffic and Road Safety Act)). Signs are sourced from Wikimedia Commons and are in the public domain.
