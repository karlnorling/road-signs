Serbia (RS)

Quick facts

Section titled “Quick facts”
Field Value
Drive side Right-hand
Language(s) Serbian
Regulator AMSS
Standard Zakon o bezbednosti saobraćaja na putevima — Vienna Convention

The @road-signs/rs package contains Serbian road signs from the Zakon o bezbednosti saobraćaja. Signs are sourced from Wikimedia Commons and are in the public domain.
